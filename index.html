Farmer Field Advisory

What You Should Do on Your Field This Season

Wheat • Sandy Loam (AWC: 112 mm)
1. Irrigation & Soil Water

Apply 30–35 mm Irrigation in 5 Days

Root-zone wetness is 0.38 with a daily deficit of -1.8 mm/day. Sandy loam soil holds 112 mm of available water; split water into two light irrigations to avoid deep leaching.

2. Recommended Crop Choice

Rotate 35% of Acreage to Drought-Hardy C4

Shifting from monoculture to Sorghum / Pearl Millet cuts water requirement from 480 mm to below 400 mm, saving ~35% of irrigation water while securing baseline harvest.

3. Thermal Stress & Weather Alert

Moderate Heat Alert (18 Days >32°C)

Canopy temperatures peak during flowering. Avoid midday flood irrigation which boils roots; irrigate in early morning or evening to preserve pollen fertility.

4. Fertilizer & Cost Savings

Intercrop Legumes: Save ~42% Synthetic Urea

Biological Nitrogen Fixation adds +65 kg N/ha into your topsoil for free. This offsets 2 bags of urea per hectare while boosting soil organic carbon.

5. Soil Cover & Groundwater Protection

Cover 3.5 Months of Bare Fallow

Sentinel-2 shows your field stays exposed for 105 days after harvest. Plant green manure (Clover, Mustard, or Vetch) to stop topsoil erosion and buffer groundwater decline (-14 mm/yr).

NASA Earth Science Data Systems (ESDS) • Open API & Data Reference

NASA Earth Observation & Data Architecture

Comprehensive specification of all satellite constellations, orbital radar sensors, radiative transfer models, and live REST API endpoints integrated across Astrosoil.

Active REST Endpoint: https://power.larc.nasa.gov/api/temporal/daily/point?community=ag&parameters=T2M_MAX,T2M_MIN,PRECTOTCORR,ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DWN,GWETTOP,GWETROOT,TS

1. NASA POWER v2.10.3 API (Prediction Of Worldwide Energy Resources)

• NASA Langley Research Center

Direct real-time HTTP client queries extracting 90-day daily point agroclimatology for localized FAO-56 Penman-Monteith Reference Evapotranspiration (ET₀), cumulative precipitation, and thermal heat wave metrics.

Endpoint: https://power.larc.nasa.gov/api/temporal/daily/point Parameters: T2M_MAX, T2M_MIN, PRECTOTCORR, ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DWN, GWETTOP, GWETROOT, TS Grid & Cadence: Global 0.5° × 0.625° reanalysis • Daily frequency • 5-day latency QA archive

2. NASA SMAP Level-4 (Soil Moisture Active Passive — SPL4SMAU)

• NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory / GSFC

Spaceborne L-band radar and radiometer assimilations integrating into the NASA Catchment Land Surface Model to output surface and subsurface root-zone soil wetness profiles, detecting agricultural drought and crop root stress.

Parameters: Root-Zone Soil Wetness (0–100 cm), Surface Soil Wetness (0–5 cm), Seasonal Drying Trend % Resolution: 9 km global grid • Multi-day orbital repeat

3. NASA GPM IMERG Final Run V07B (Global Precipitation Measurement)

• NASA Goddard Space Flight Center / JAXA

Inter-calibrated multi-satellite precipitation algorithm merging dual-frequency precipitation radar, passive microwave, and geostationary infrared observations to measure cumulative rainfall, deluge events (>110 mm/day), and historical seasonal anomalies.

Parameters: Summer Season Accumulation (mm), Precipitation Anomaly, Heavy Rain Days (>110 mm) Resolution: 11 km (0.1° × 0.1°) • Monthly & Half-hourly products

4. NASA MODIS Terra & Aqua (MOD13Q1 / MOD16A2GF / MOD11A2)

• NASA Land Processes DAAC

Establishes Astrosoil's common 250 m harmonization benchmark for canopy vegetative vigor through Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI), transpirational ET/PET water demand ratios, and Land Surface Temperature (LST).

Parameters: MOD13Q1 250 m 16-day NDVI, MOD16A2GF 500 m 8-day ET/PET ratio, MOD11A2 1 km LST Coverage: Global 250 m Harmonization Target • 2000–Present archive

5. NASA ECOSTRESS (AppEEARS / International Space Station)

• NASA JPL / International Space Station

High-resolution thermal radiometer operating from the ISS to measure field-level plant temperatures, computing the Evaporative Stress Index (ESI) and identifying midday heat-induced stomatal closure before visible crop wilting.

Parameters: Evaporative Stress Index (ESI), Evaporative Fraction, Local Canopy Thermal Stress Resolution: 70 m field precision • Precessing ISS orbital cadence

6. NASA GRACE / GRACE-FO Tellus Mascons

• NASA JPL / GFZ German Research Centre

Tracks microscopic variations in Earth's gravitational pull to measure decadal Terrestrial Water Storage (TWS) anomalies and regional deep groundwater depletion rates in mm/year, evaluating tubewell sustainability.

Parameters: Terrestrial Water Storage (TWS) Mass Anomaly, Groundwater Depletion Trend (mm/yr) Dataset: JPL Mascon RL06 • 2002–Present timeseries

7. NASA GLDAS Noah Land Surface Model 2.1

• NASA GES DISC

Simulates global land hydrology and energy fluxes at 3-hourly time-steps to compute cumulative growing degree days (GDD), critical nocturnal frost risks, and heat wave spells above 35°C during sensitive crop flowering stages.

Parameters: Growing Degree Days (GDD, base 10°C), Consecutive Heat Wave Days (>35°C), Late Frost Hazard Resolution: 0.25° spatial grid • 3-hourly physics assimilation

8. NASA SRTM v3.0 Global 1-ArcSecond

• NASA JPL / National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency

Void-filled radar interferometric digital elevation model providing 30 m terrain elevation, slope gradients, and Topographic Position Index (TPI) to assess soil erosion risk, water runoff, and gravity-fed irrigation options.

Parameters: Elevation (m a.s.l.), Slope Percentage (%), Topographic Position Index (TPI) Resolution: 30 m (1 arc-second) global coverage

9. ISRIC SoilGrids 2.0 & European Commission JRC EUCROPMAP

• World Soil Information & JRC

Machine learning global soil mapping determining sand/clay proportions, Soil Organic Carbon (SOC), pH, and Available Water Capacity (AWC), coupled with multi-year satellite crop classifications to match crop species to field conditions.

Parameters: Sand & Clay Fractions %, Soil Organic Carbon (SOC g/kg), pH (H₂O), Available Water Capacity (AWC mm) Resolution: 250 m global predictions • 0–30 cm topsoil profile

10. DWD Climate Data Center Ground Check (Cottbus Station)

• Deutscher Wetterdienst • Station ID 00880 • Open Data CC BY 4.0

In-situ meteorological station ground-truth validation verifying NASA POWER accuracy (r = 0.96, bias +0.3°C, 98.2% match) to provide verified scientific confidence for farm managers.

Parameters: Ground Station Air Temperature, Precipitation, Solar Radiation Verification Station: Cottbus (51.7606°N, 14.3329°E) • Reference Benchmark
