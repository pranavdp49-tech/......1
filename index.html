Agro-Ecological Diagnosis

The Problem: Why the Farmer Is Struggling

Continuous single-crop farming under compounding climate stress causes systemic yield loss, water exhaustion, and depleted soil biology.

1. Volatile Weather

Weather is changing: increased heat waves, thermal stress spikes, and unpredictable rainfall timing during crucial vegetative windows.

Observation: NASA POWER 2m Air Temp & GPM Precip

2. Vanishing Water

Water is running out: underground aquifers and topsoil moisture reserves are critically depleting under intensive irrigation demands.

Observation: SMAP L4 GWETTOP Surface Wetness

3. Tired, Mining Soil

Tired soil: planting the same crop season after season drains the nitrogen reserve, degrades organic carbon, and leaves fields exposed to erosion.

Observation: MODIS NDVI Vegetative Ground Cover

Strategy C: Legume Intercrop
